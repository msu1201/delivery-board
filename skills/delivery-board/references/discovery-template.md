# Project discovery worksheet

Copy into the project's `.delivery-board/DISCOVERY.md`. Replace the prompts with findings as the conversation progresses. Keep unanswered items as unknown; omit sections that do not apply.

## Baseline state

Draft / confirmed; review date and revision or hash. For a confirmed baseline, record the user's actual decision and exclusions. Planning agreement does not establish product acceptance.

## Goal and first useful version

- Who uses this, and what result do they need?
- What belongs in the first version? What is outside it?
- What constraints would change the plan?

## Decisions still needed

List only questions that materially affect scope, order or acceptance. Keep answered questions with their decisions so another session can resume.

## Review record

Record the concrete route shown, draft hash/revision, user's decision, unresolved risks accepted into the baseline and excluded work. While awaiting a decision, write “Awaiting route confirmation.” Never supply a decision on the user's behalf.

## Maintenance

Name the authoritative source, who maintains it, the checker command and how approved changes reach the graph. Preserve verification, acceptance and delivery separately.
